## Practical tools. Shared progress.

OctoKon is a software organization exploring developer tools and AI-assisted development. We bring research, ideas, and engineering together to build useful software.

## Current work

### [Octo-Harness ↗](https://github.com/OctoKon/Octo-Harness)

Research and development of a unified coding-agent CLI and harness.

The project is in its early research and planning stage. Follow the repository for its direction, documentation, and progress.

## Our approach

- **Start with the problem.** Keep the purpose clear and the solution understandable.
- **Make progress reviewable.** Work in focused changes and document the decisions behind them.
- **Build together.** Welcome useful questions, different perspectives, and thoughtful contributions.
